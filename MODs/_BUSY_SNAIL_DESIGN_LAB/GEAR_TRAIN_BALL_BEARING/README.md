# Busy Snail Filament Changer (BSFC) Drive Unit <ins> Rewind Geartrain Ball Bearing Mod </ins> by Busy Snail Design Lab (README WIP 2026-07-28)

Possibly a completely useless mod that only extends the BOM. Rotating polymer parts lubricated with silicone grease are a widely used solution in low speed, light load applications like this. Ball bearings might make this a little quieter. However, high quality ball bearings are quite expensive, some of the cheapest ones on Ali even lack grease inside.

The two drive unit blocks and two gears shown in the pictures are different from the standard ones. A few drops of superglue will help hold the flanged F6700zz bearing in place.
